About Us

The Franklin Lakes Chapter of Project Smile was founded by students who wanted to make a real impact in their community. What started as a small toy drive quickly grew into a larger effort to support children in hospitals and bring comfort during difficult times.

Our Officers

Nina Guttilla

Nina Guttilla is the President of the Franklin Lakes Chapter of Project Smile, where she leads efforts to organize toy drives and expand the organization’s impact within the community. Driven by a passion for helping others, Nina founded the chapter to bring comfort and joy to children in hospitals through simple acts of kindness. She oversees outreach, partnerships, and event coordination, ensuring each initiative runs smoothly and reaches as many families as possible. Under her leadership, the chapter continues to grow, inspiring more students to get involved and make a meaningful difference.

Robert Tanis

Robert Tanis serves as the Marketing President of the Franklin Lakes Chapter of Project Smile, where he leads branding, outreach, and digital strategy. He focuses on growing awareness through social media, community engagement, and creative campaigns that inspire people to get involved. With a strong interest in technology and media, Robert helps the organization connect with a wider audience and maximize its impact. His work ensures that every toy drive reaches more supporters—and ultimately brings more smiles to kids who need it most.

Donation Guidelines

To keep our patients safe, we can only accept new, unopened items purchased within the last year due to their immunocompromised conditions.

Baby & Toddler

Rattles, musical toys, manipulatives

School-Age Children

Dolls, LEGO, board games, craft kits

Teens

Accessories, electronics, gift cards

Additional Requested Items

  • Baby toys
  • Teen items and electronics
  • Bandages and basic care items (like Band-Aids)
  • Play-Doh and sensory toys
  • Pajamas and clothing (including socks and underwear)
  • Books, crafts, and coloring supplies

Your support helps us bring comfort, joy, and normalcy to our patients—thank you for making a difference.
